🔤 Pre-A1 · Unit 1: The Alphabet & Spelling

Learn the 26 letter names in American English. Listen, repeat, read, and practise spelling.

Spanish support with simple English examples.

1. Alphabet · Listen to each letter

Tap a letter to hear its name and an example word.

2. Listen and repeat

Listen to the alphabet in groups, then repeat aloud.

Audio uses your browser's built-in American English voice when available.

3. Reading · A spelling conversation

At a reception desk
Receptionist: What's your name?
Guest: My name is Alex Morgan.
Receptionist: Could you spell your last name, please?
Guest: M-O-R-G-A-N.
Receptionist: Thank you!

Check understanding: How do you spell Morgan?

4. Speaking Studio · Record yourself

Say the letters in your name. Then listen to your recording and try again.

Model: “My name is Alex. A-L-E-X.”

Allow microphone access when your browser asks.

5. Writing · Spell a word

Listen to the word, then type the correct spelling.

6. Quick review

Which letter comes after M?

🔢 Pre-A1 · Unit 2: Numbers

Learn to recognize, say, read, and write numbers from 0 to 100.

1. Numbers · 0–20

Tap each number to hear it in American English.

2. Tens · 20–100

Listen and repeat. Notice the difference between thirteen and thirty.

3. Reading · At a café

Server: How many coffees would you like?
Customer: Two, please.
Server: Anything else?
Customer: Yes, three bottles of water.
Server: That's twelve dollars and fifty cents.
Customer: Here you are.

Check understanding: How many bottles of water does the customer want?

4. Listening · Number dictation

Listen to the number. Type it using digits.

5. Speaking · Phone numbers

Listen to the model. Then say your own phone number aloud, one digit at a time.

Model: My phone number is 482-7319.

6. Writing · Numbers in words

Listen, then write the number using English words.

🎨 Pre-A1 · Unit 3: Colors, Shapes & Classroom Objects

First, learn the words. Listen, repeat, read, speak, and write. Grammar will come after we build our vocabulary.

1. Vocabulary · Colors

Tap each word to hear its American English pronunciation.

2. Vocabulary · Shapes & classroom objects

Listen and repeat. Notice the word and its meaning.

3. Reading · In the classroom

Teacher: What color is the book?
Student: It is blue.
Teacher: And what shape is the clock?
Student: It is round.
Teacher: Please put your pencil on the desk.
Student: Okay!

What color is the book?

4. Listening · Choose the word

Listen. Which word do you hear?

5. Speaking · Describe your classroom

Listen to the model. Then describe two objects around you.

Model: The book is blue. The clock is round.

6. Writing · Spell the word

Listen and write the word in English.

7. Quick review

Which shape has three sides?

🌎 Pre-A1 · Unit 4: Countries & Nationalities

Learn country names and nationality words. Listen carefully to pronunciation and notice capital letters.

1. Vocabulary · Countries

Tap a country to hear its name.

2. Vocabulary · Nationalities

Listen and repeat the nationality words.

3. Reading · An international class

Hello! My name is Mia. I am from Canada. I am Canadian.
My friend is Carlos. He is from Mexico. He is Mexican.
Our teacher is Ana. She is from Brazil. She is Brazilian.

Where is Carlos from?

4. Listening · Identify the country

Listen to the country name and choose it.

5. Speaking · Introduce your country

Listen to the model. Then say your country and nationality.

Model: I am from Guatemala. I am Guatemalan.

6. Writing · Complete the nationality

Listen to the nationality and type it correctly.

7. Quick review

Which nationality is connected to Japan?

💼 Pre-A1 · Unit 5: Jobs & Occupations

Discover common occupations, workplaces, and useful words for talking about work. We will study grammar later.

1. Vocabulary · Jobs

Tap each job to hear its American English pronunciation.

2. Vocabulary · Workplaces & tools

Learn where people work and what they use.

3. Reading · Meet the workers

Emma is a doctor. She works at a hospital.
Ben is a teacher. He works at a school.
Leo is a chef. He works in a restaurant.
Sara is a police officer. She works at a police station.

Where does Ben work?

4. Listening · Guess the job

Listen to the job and choose the matching word.

5. Speaking · Talk about a job

Listen to the model. Then choose a job and say where this person works.

Model: A nurse works at a hospital.

6. Writing · Spell the occupation

Listen and type the job in English.

7. Quick review

Who works in a restaurant and prepares food?
